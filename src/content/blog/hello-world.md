I finally got around to building a place to write things down. This is it.

I spend most of my days in infrastructure — Kubernetes, pipelines, the glue that
keeps platforms running quietly in the background. A lot of what I learn never
leaves a Slack thread or a postmortem doc. The plan for this blog is to fix that.

## What to expect

- **Infra notes** — things I wish I'd known earlier about Kubernetes, GitOps,
  Terraform, and running services at scale.
- **Build logs** — short write-ups as I work on side projects.
- **Opinions** — occasionally, and clearly labelled as such.

No fixed schedule, no fluff. If something here saves you an afternoon, that's a win.
